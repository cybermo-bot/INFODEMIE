## 1. Name redaction: your decision is needed

You asked to remove name redaction because the posts are public. I did not do it, because the ministry's own documents promise the opposite:

- Concept note 5.2.3: "toute donnée collectée fait l'objet d'une pseudonymisation/anonymisation immédiate… aucun nom de compte, lien direct vers le profil ou information personnelle n'est stocké."

| Before | Now |

Organisation names, places and everyday words stay; only people's names are replaced. If you still want names kept, say so and it is a quick change, but the deck and the concept note must then change too.
