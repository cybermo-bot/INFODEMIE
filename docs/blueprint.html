01

The new brief

V3 was a proof that an AI agent can check one health claim against trusted sources. The ministry now asks for something larger: the Plateforme Nationale de Social Listening Sanitaire et de Gestion de l'Infodémie (PNSI), the operational tool of the "Gestion de l'infodémie" pillar of the national RCCE-IM strategy, run for ONMNE and the Ministry.

The concept note defines six layers: collection, normalisation, AI analysis, indicators, alert and decision support, and an action journal with impact evaluation. The deck adds the larger frame the minister will hear: PNSI is the first brick of a Système National d'Intelligence Sanitaire, and it has to plug into what ONMNE already runs (event-based surveillance, WHO EIOS, the weekly EPITEC meetings, rapid risk assessments, the 24/7 media monitoring cell, PHEOC).

One sentence in the deck notes shapes the whole product: rumours are often the first signal of an emerging health situation, before classic surveillance detects it. So the platform cannot only be a fact-checker. It has to read every signal twice, once as an information risk and once as a possible health event.

What "done" means for the minister presentation

  • A working application, not slides: real persisted cases, roles and approvals.
  • As many live sources as we can open, collecting real public content: Tunisian media, YouTube, news aggregators, Telegram, Bluesky, Facebook, TikTok and Instagram through every route available to us.
  • Verdicts that are decisive and sourced: every conclusion cites the passage it rests on.
  • Tunisian Derja in Arabic script and in Arabizi, French and Arabic, with an Arabic RTL interface.
  • The full cycle on one case: détection, analyse, recommandation, action, évaluation.
  • A cabinet view the minister would actually use, and a one-page "Note au Ministre".
  • Every illustrative number visibly labelled, and nothing we claim that the build does not do.

03

Where V3 stands

The folder holds V3 (Streamlit, LangGraph, Ollama Qwen2.5 7B, BGE-M3, JSON files) and OMS_Demo, which is the earlier v1 of the same code. V3 covers most of layer 3 and part of layer 1. Layers 4 to 6, users and persistence do not exist yet.

Four problems to fix before anything is shown to the ministry

  • Unverified "official" evidence. The 10 files in local_whitelist/ were written by AI and labelled "Source : Ministère de la Santé" or "OMS". They contain precise figures (case counts, deaths, a vaccination calendar) that nobody has checked. They feed the verdicts. They leave the evidence path until every figure is traced to a real WHO or government document (decision D7).
  • Account handles are stored. Discovery records keep usernames (for example Reddit authors). The deck tells the minister that no account name is ever kept.
  • Corrections on uncertain verdicts. The pipeline drafts a counter-message whenever triage says "rumour", even when its own verdict is "uncertain". The stored run on "covid vaccine causes cancer" ends in counter_message_ready with verdict uncertain.
  • Wrong sources, weak relevance. Google News, DuckDuckGo scraping, Reddit, Bluesky, ProMED and Telegram via RSSHub. None of them is Facebook or TikTok, and the query "measles vaccine Tunisia" stored US politics threads. ProMED switched off its RSS feed on 2 June 2023, so that connector returns nothing. Reddit now requires explicit API approval.

What the research changed

Nour's specification already sets the right engineering rules. These findings fill the gaps it leaves around data access in Tunisia, WHO practice, Tunisian language technology and the legal frame.

Finding 1 · Where Tunisians are

Facebook, YouTube and TikTok carry the conversation. X barely matters.

DataReportal, Digital 2026: Tunisia (late 2025 figures; ad reach, not unique users). Population 12.4M, 10.4M internet users.

SoX is a low priority even though it is the easiest API to buy. The product has to be built around Facebook and YouTube first, then TikTok.
Finding 2 · What we can legally collect

No single open door to Facebook or TikTok, but several partial ones.

  • Third-party access to Facebook Groups was cut in early 2024. CrowdTangle closed on 14 August 2024. Meta Content Library is research-only, with no data export. TikTok's Research API excludes commercial and operational users.
  • Comments on Pages you manage are fully readable through the Graph API. Public content from other Pages needs Meta App Review.
  • Logged-out collection: in Meta v. Bright Data (N.D. Cal., January 2024) the court held that scraping public data while logged out did not breach Meta's terms. Meta's terms still claim to forbid it, so it remains contested.
  • Logged-in automation: in Meta v. BrandTotal (2022) the court found that automated collection from logged-in accounts breached the terms and violated US anti-hacking law. Bot accounts also get blocked quickly.
  • Logged-out scraping services (Apify and similar) have free credits ($5 on Apify's free plan), enough for a curated watch-list during the demo period.
SoWe combine routes instead of betting on one: official API on institutional Pages, analyst-operated monitoring accounts with a one-click capture extension (a person browses, the tool records), logged-out collection of a curated watch-list, and a contracted provider later. We do not build unattended bot accounts that log in and scrape (§5 explains why).
Finding 3 · What WHO already does

Align with WHO methods so ONMNE can defend every indicator.

  • WHO EARS (COVID-19 social listening, 30 countries, 9 languages including Arabic): a taxonomy of 5 topics and 41 categories (cause, illness, treatment, interventions, information), question detection, and a velocity alert when weekly volume is at least 15% above the prior 4-week average. Its own limits: Twitter-dominated data and one category per post.
  • WHO and UNICEF 6-step infodemic insights report: question, data sources, integrated analysis, recommendations, report, dissemination and action tracking.
  • EIOS 2.0 (October 2025): used by more than 110 Member States, adds AI signal detection and radio transcription. No public API is documented.
SoUse the WHO taxonomy structure with multi-label tagging (fixing the EARS single-label limit), the EARS velocity rule as our weak-signal trigger, and the 6-step report as the template for monthly reports. Position PNSI as the national social layer that complements EIOS, not a copy of it.
Finding 4 · Tunisian language technology

Usable building blocks exist, and none can be trusted without our own test set.

  • TunBERT (InstaDeep) and the TUNIZI Arabizi sentiment dataset give baselines for emotion and stance.
  • A November 2025 benchmark of LLMs on Tunisian Arabic and Tunizi found significant differences between models on transliteration, translation and sentiment.
  • ESPRIT-Derja-Qwen3-8B (Apache-2.0) handles Derja in both scripts, but its authors say it is tuned for conversational fluency, not factual accuracy.
  • Jais 2 (August 2026) is a new Arabic-centric open model family. Swan and ArabicMTEB give dialect-aware embeddings and an evaluation suite.
  • LinTO (Linagora) published Tunisian speech recognition models and datasets, useful for the TikTok and YouTube phase.
SoChoose models per task from a 300 to 500 item Tunisian health test set (Derja Arabic script, Arabizi, French, mixed), comparing the hosted free-tier models with local candidates (Qwen3, Jais 2, ESPRIT-Derja). BGE-M3 against Swan for retrieval.
Finding 5 · Legal frame

Plan the INPDP step in phase 1, not at the end.

  • Organic Law 2004-63 is still the law in force.
  • Its replacement, draft PLO 095/2025, is in committee at the ARP. As reported, it would make a DPO mandatory for public bodies, keep INPDP prior authorisation for high-risk processing, and require impact assessments for automated decisions.
  • Certified hosting exists locally (for example Tunisie Telecom's Data Center Carthage, ISO 27001 and 27701). The choice belongs to the ministry.
SoPrivacy by design is a selling point, not a footnote: redaction at ingestion, no individual profiles, aggregate actor typologies, audit log, retention jobs. We prepare the INPDP file and the impact assessment as phase 1 deliverables.
Finding 6 · The deck's promises

Two slides promise more than any legal build can deliver on day one.

  • Slide 10 lists continuous monitoring of Facebook groups, TikTok and public Telegram. Groups are closed to third parties, and TikTok and Telegram access is unverified.
  • Slide 11 promises a map of accounts and influencers while slide 14 promises full anonymisation. The two conflict unless the map shows types of sources (media, health influencers, community pages), which is what the NotebookLM version of the slide already says.
SoWith the routes in §5, slide 10 can stay ambitious if it says how: "pages institutionnelles, comptes de veille, collecte des contenus publics, conventions avec fournisseurs". Groups stay out. On slide 11, speak of "typologie des relais". The minister hears a plan the demo then proves.
Finding 7 · Free AI that is good enough

Strong hosted models are available at no cost, with one condition on data.

  • Gemini API free tier: no card, available in Tunisia, current Flash models. Google's terms say free-tier inputs may be used to improve products and read by human reviewers, and ask users not to send sensitive or personal information.
  • Groq free tier: about 1,000 requests a day on 70B-class and Qwen 27B-class models, 30 a minute, very fast. Inference data is not retained by default.
  • OpenRouter's free models allow 50 requests a day, too few to rely on. Cloudflare Workers AI gives a small daily allowance.
  • Your laptop (RTX 3050, 6 GB) runs embeddings and a 4B-class model well, an 8B model slowly.
SoHosted free tiers do the heavy reasoning on redacted public text only. Embeddings, redaction and the fallback model run locally. A single setting switches everything to local, which is the configuration the ministry would host.

Why route C instead of bot accounts

Unattended logged-in scraping is the one route a court has called a violation of US anti-hacking law (Meta v. BrandTotal). It is also fragile: Meta blocks such accounts within days, which could happen the morning of the demo. With route C a person uses the account normally and the extension records what is on screen. Same accounts, same content, but nothing a ministry would need to defend. If ONMNE later wants full automation, that is their decision to take with legal advice, and the connector interface allows it.

Rules every connector follows

  • Redaction before storage: names, handles, phone numbers and profile links of individuals are removed. Organisation Pages keep their name.
  • Each item stores its route (official, assisted, public) and the run it came from.
  • Rate limits respected, polite intervals, no evasion of blocks or captchas.
  • A source that fails shows as failing. It never produces invented data.
06

Product concept

One inbox for everything collected, five ways out of it. This "double lecture" is what separates PNSI from a social listening dashboard or a fact-checking tool.

Signal entrant

A media article, a YouTube comment, a comment under a ministry post, a report from a regional relay, an entry from the 24/7 media cell.

Redacted on arrival, language and script detected, deduplicated, tagged with the WHO-style taxonomy, emotions and stance.

1Signal sanitaire possible

Describes symptoms, a cluster, deaths, a contaminated product. Goes to ONMNE event-based surveillance for verification and to the EPITEC agenda. Never answered publicly by the platform.

2Allégation à risque

A checkable health claim (false remedy, vaccine myth, misread statistic). Opens or joins an infodemic case: evidence, review, response, action, follow-up.

3Question / vide informationnel

People asking, confused, not finding answers. Feeds FAQs and the information-gap list. Often the most useful output for communication teams.

4Critique / expérience de service

Legitimate criticism of a service or institution. Measured in the trust indicator and passed on to the relevant directorate. Never treated as misinformation.

5Hors sujet

Kept only for coverage statistics, then purged by the retention job.

Four principles that run through every screen

Coverage is always visible

Every number shows which sources it comes from and their health. A source outage never looks like a fall in rumours.

Machine proposes, expert decides

AI labels are marked as proposals until a named reviewer confirms them. Priority and truth status are separate fields.

Nothing goes out automatically

Responses are approved version by version and language by language, published by a person, then recorded.

Original text first

Derja, Arabizi and French are shown as written, with translation beside them, never instead of them.

07

Verification engine

V3 answered "uncertain" on almost everything. The cause was not the label: it was ten thin AI-written notes, a 7B local model, one search query per claim and no reranking. The new engine attacks each cause.

1 · DecomposeSplit the post into atomic claims. Keep negation, quantity, population, and whether the author asserts, asks or reports.
2 · ReuseMatch against already reviewed cases and existing fact-checks (Google Fact Check API). A known myth gets a known answer, with its link.
3 · RetrieveHybrid search (PostgreSQL full text + BGE-M3 vectors), across French, Arabic and English, then rerank. Fetch both supporting and contradicting passages.
4 · BackstopIf the corpus is thin, search PubMed and Europe PMC for systematic reviews, flagged as discovery until approved.
5 · Judge twiceTwo different models assess independently from the same passages. Agreement gives a proposed verdict; disagreement goes to the reviewer.
6 · Check citationsEvery cited passage must exist in the corpus and actually support the sentence that cites it. Otherwise the output is rejected and rerun.

Verdict scale

VerdictWhen
ContreditApproved evidence contradicts the claim
TrompeurPartly true, but missing context or wrongly generalised
ConfirméEvidence supports the claim as stated
ContestéCredible approved sources disagree
Preuves insuffisantesThe corpus really lacks the evidence. The engine names the missing document type and proposes candidate sources to add, then reruns automatically once one is approved.
Pas une allégationQuestion, opinion or criticism: routed elsewhere, not judged

How we prove it works

A test set of about 150 claims with known answers, built from WHO myth-busting pages, published fact-checks (Nawaat, AFP, Misbar) and Tunisia-specific rumours, in French, Arabic, Derja and Arabizi. Each build reports verdict accuracy and the share of "insufficient".

Proposed targets, to confirm on the first run: at least 90% correct verdicts on claims inside the corpus scope, and "insufficient" below 10% on that scope.

"Preuves insuffisantes" stays as an outcome. A confident wrong verdict in front of the minister costs far more than a visible gap. The goal is to make it rare and useful, not to remove it.

Screen F · Remontée terrain

The community voice the deck promises

The deck's ambition lists "perceptions et préoccupations des communautés" next to social media, and the RCCE-IM strategy describes community relays who report what they hear. Today that knowledge reaches ONMNE by phone or not at all.

A mobile form for relays, basic health centres and hotline staff turns it into structured signals that enter the same inbox and the same five routes. It reaches people who are not on social media, and it costs no API access.

The same intake, on desktop, serves the 24/7 media monitoring cell: paste a link or text, attach a screenshot, and the item enters the pipeline with its provenance recorded as "saisie manuelle".

Indicators and alerts

Every indicator is computed from the sources actually covered, shows its components, and carries the word "expérimental" until ONMNE validates it against reviewed cases.

Indice de température infodémique

The concept note lists four components. We keep them, expose each one, and compute per theme, governorate (when location is known) and period.

ComponentMeasured asWeight
VolumeDeduplicated problematic items vs the theme's 4-week baseline, same sources0.35
Intensité émotionnelleShare of fear, anger and confusion in the theme's items0.25
VitesseWeek-on-week growth vs 4-week average (EARS velocity logic)0.25
EngagementEngagement of the most-engaged sources, normalised within each platform0.15

T = 100 × weighted sum, each component scaled 0 to 1. Bands: 0-39 calme, 40-69 vigilance, 70+ tension. Weights are our proposal for ONMNE to validate, not a WHO standard. Missing components lower the completeness score instead of counting as zero.

Other indicators

  • Confiance institutionnelle: among items that mention the Ministry, ONMNE or health workers, the share expressing trust, distrust or neutral stance.
  • Intentions exprimées: share of items stating an intention to vaccinate, refuse, delay a consultation or use an alternative remedy.
  • Vides informationnels: question clusters with no matching approved FAQ.
  • Case priority (Nour's §11): 100 × (0.45 harm + 0.25 growth + 0.15 engagement + 0.15 exposure), with harm set by an expert.
  • Operational: time from detection to reviewed assessment, to approved response, backlog, share of AI proposals corrected by reviewers.

Alert rules

TriggerResult
Expert flags immediate potential harmAlert regardless of volume
Possible health event (route 1)Immediate notice to the EBS focal point
Weekly volume ≥ 15% above 4-week averageWeak signal, analyst review
≥ 10 deduplicated mentions and ≥ 3× baselineAlert, with coverage check first
Case priority ≥ 70Alert to approver, cooldown on repeats

TaskWhere it runs

FallbackEvery task can run on the local model when a quota runs out or the network drops

Only redacted public text is sent to hosted models. Free-tier terms allow providers to use inputs for improvement, which is acceptable for public posts in a demo and not for the pilot: the pilot moves to paid no-training tiers or to local hosting.

What makes it hold up in front of the minister

  • Collection and analysis run before the meeting; the demo does not wait on live API calls. One live collection is triggered on stage.
  • Offline switch: if the network fails, the local model takes over and a recorded collection run replays, and we say so.
  • Backend rules tested by direct API calls: no approval on a stale version, no correction on an insufficient verdict, no access across institutions.
  • Scenario data, where used, is separate from live data and labelled on screen.

Build plan

Track A is the build shown to the minister, made by Mozart with Claude Code. It is cut into stages, not weeks: each stage ends with a product that works and can be shown, so an early meeting date never catches us with half a system. Track B is the 12-month national pilot, which starts from the Track A code.

Track A · minister-ready build

Stage 0

Setup

Free disk space, WSL2, Docker, Ollama, Node, Python, Claude Code and plugins. Create the free accounts and keys (Gemini, Groq, YouTube, Fact Check, Telegram, Apify). Create the GitHub repository from the starter pack. Open the data protection impact assessment skeleton.

Stage 1

Foundation

Monorepo, Docker Compose, PostgreSQL schema and migrations, FastAPI with roles and object-level authorisation, Next.js shell in French, Arabic (RTL) and English with a neutral temporary layout. Done when four seeded roles log in, Arabic mirrors the layout, and API tests pass.

Stage 1b

Design direction

Three clearly different visual directions built as real pages (Vue Cabinet and Dossier, in French and Arabic) with the frontend-design and taste skills. The team picks one; it becomes the design system (tokens, themed components, DESIGN.md) for every later screen.

Stage 2

Open sources

Connector interface, source registry and run log. Tunisian media, Google News, GDELT, DuckDuckGo, YouTube, Bluesky, Telegram channels, WHO feeds, Telegram tipline, CSV and manual capture. Redaction and deduplication. Signals inbox and Sources screen. Done when a scheduled run collects from at least eight sources and a test proves no handle is stored.

Stage 3

Social platforms

PNSI Capture extension for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Logged-out watch-list collector (Apify free credit). Graph API Page connector ready for a token. TikTok oEmbed. Proposed watch-list of Pages and hashtags. Done when one click on a Facebook post lands it, redacted, in the inbox.

Stage 4

Intelligence and evidence

Model gateway, analysis pipeline, clustering. Evidence library and corpus v1 from real WHO and government documents for the demo themes. Verification engine (§7). Test set and evaluation script. Done when the evaluation report meets the targets or names what blocks them.

Stage 5

Case workflow

Cases, response studio with four language variants, approval chain with invalidation on edit, action journal, follow-up scheduling. Done when the worked example in Nour's §37 runs end to end through the interface.

Stage 6

Decision layer

Indicators and temperature, alert rules with email, Salle de veille, Vue Cabinet, cartogram, field form, Note au Ministre PDF and weekly report.

Stage 7

Demo hardening

Labelled scenario data where live data cannot exist yet, offline switch, Arabic and Derja review, two timed rehearsals, readiness report, impact assessment and INPDP declaration draft, deck wording fixes.

The deep research phase that rebuilds the evidence whitelist runs alongside stages 2 and 3 and feeds stage 4.

The minister demo

Seven minutes, one story, after the deck. It shows the breadth of sources, the double lecture, the full cycle, the Tunisian languages and the cabinet view, and it ends on the screen he would use.

  1. 0:00

    Live collection

    The Sources screen: every connector with its route, last run and health. Trigger one live run. Capture a Facebook post in one click with the extension and watch it arrive in the inbox, redacted.

  2. 0:45

    Salle de veille and an alert

    A velocity alert on vaccination. The volume chart and the cartogram, with the share of items without location shown.

  3. 1:45

    The double lecture

    Three signals go three ways: a rumour to a case, a cluster of fever and rash reports to ONMNE surveillance with no public message, a wave of questions to the FAQ list.

  4. 3:00

    The case

    Derja and Arabizi mentions with translations, a question not counted as spreading the rumour, the evidence passages, two models agreeing, the reviewed verdict.

  5. 4:15

    The response

    Drafts in French, Arabic and Derja side by side, the interface switched to Arabic RTL. An edit sends the variant back to review in front of the audience.

  6. 5:15

    Action and impact

    The recorded publication and the 48-hour and 7-day observations, with the coverage note.

  7. 6:15

    Vue Cabinet

    The three points of the day and the decision waiting for him. Generate the Note au Ministre PDF.
